Tide records at key gauges

Highest observed water levels, feet above MLLW, pulled from NOAA CO-OPS verified data for the three events that set the modern marks: January 1983 (El Niño storm), January 2005 (king tide plus storm), and November 2015 (El Niño king tide, the official record at San Diego, La Jolla and Santa Barbara per the California Ocean Protection Council). Santa Barbara has no verified data for January 2005 (gauge gap). Los Angeles' highest value among the events checked is January 2005; the 1983 peak there was 7.82 ft. Santa Monica's record is not confirmed by an official source; 7.85 ft (Jan 10, 2005) is the highest in the events checked. The NOAA "top 10 water levels" tab in the Inundation Dashboard is the authoritative list; it is linked under Live links.

Surge plus tide guidance (P-ETSS)

The NWS probabilistic surge model you flagged. These pages render in the browser, so they open in a new tab with your settings preserved. Read the 10 percent exceedance line as the reasonable worst case and the 50 percent line as the expected case. P-ETSS is extratropical guidance and does not model Rachel's wave setup, so add runup on top.
